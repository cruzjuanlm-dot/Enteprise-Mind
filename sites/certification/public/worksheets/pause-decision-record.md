# Pause decision record

Flux prototype worksheet. Fictional where filled. Not an accredited record.

The sponsor and the steward sign this before an AI or enterprise project starts. The signature is the decision. A meeting invite is not.

## Record

- Organization:
- Project the pause covers:
- Date (UTC):
- Standard version the organization will learn:
- What will not start until this pause ends:
- What the organization does not yet have a shared word for:
- Sponsor, name and signature:
- Steward, name and signature:

## Filled example (fictional)

- Organization: Hearthline Instruments, Field Systems
- Project: Kilnwatch (`project.kilnwatch`)
- Date: 2026-11-01 (UTC)
- Standard version: Flux Standard v0.1 (Draft)
- What will not start: a new workflow that writes firing curves into a second system, and any capture project, until the language course is finished and this record is signed.
- What is not yet shared: the difference between a firing curve (the record) and a recording of the room (not the record). See unit `kilnwatch-seed-DEC-001`.
- Sponsor: not yet signed
- Steward: not yet signed

This example is not signed. The Big Pause for Kilnwatch is not closed.
