# Unit-capture card

Flux prototype worksheet. Fictional where filled. Not an accredited record.

One card, one unit. The card is the capture. The Markdown file is the record. Do not paste a transcript onto the card.

## Card

- Working title:
- Type (DEC, REQ, ARC, DS, BR, WF, OQ):
- Scope (project or company):
- Project id:
- Owner (person-role id):
- The statement, in sentences:
- What this unit does not claim:
- Supersedes (id, or none):
- Register: asset or debt, and why:

## Filled example (fictional)

Taken from the accepted unit `kilnwatch-seed-DEC-001`.

- Working title: Firing recordings stay out of the database
- Type: DEC
- Scope: project
- Project id: project.kilnwatch
- Owner: person-role.catalog-editor
- Statement: Hearthline does not keep firing recordings. A studio seeing its own curve does not require a stored session. The firing-curve data source is the record.
- Does not claim: that entity rows may never be mirrored, or that a studio may not see its own curve.
- Supersedes: none
- Register: asset. The decision is accepted and filed.
