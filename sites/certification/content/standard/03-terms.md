**Knowledge.** A fact, decision, procedure, or constraint that is not yet a governed record. In the teaching story, a firing curve is knowledge. A recording of the room is not a substitute for the curve.

**Unit.** One governed Markdown record, with frontmatter, one type, an owner, and a file in git. Type codes in the teaching release are DEC, REQ, ARC, DS, BR, WF, and OQ.

**Comp.** A versioned container that points at units and carries a registry id. A course in this catalog is a comp. The site and the tool read the same kind of id.

**Organized.** The unit has a type, an owner, a scope, and a row on a register. An unwritten agreement is not organized.

**Actionable value.** An accepted unit someone can carry out without asking the room what was meant.

**Velocity.** The elapsed time, in days, from knowledge arriving to an accepted unit that changes the work. A count of meetings is not velocity.

**Enterprise debt.** Knowledge the organization depends on that is not organized. An unowned decision, a procedure that lives in one person, a register that does not match the files. Debt is a drag on velocity. It is not a verdict on the people.

**Big Pause.** The signed decision to stop, learn a common language, and agree terms before an AI or enterprise project starts.

**Value session.** A meeting held to capture typed knowledge, such as a decision or a risk, into units. It is not a status review.

**Asset register.** The list of units and other governed assets the organization means to keep.

**Debt register.** The list of known enterprise debt, each row with an owner and a disposition.

**Readiness.** Five scores, each from 0 to 5: common language, asset register, debt register, value sessions, and certified people. The board sees the five numbers. There is no single badge that replaces them.
