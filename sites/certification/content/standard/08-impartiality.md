The body that teaches and the body that certifies are not the same body.

The Standards Council maintains the text of this standard.

The Certification Board maintains the marks: the blueprint, issuance, renewal, and revocation.

A licensed trainer may deliver a course. That trainer does not sit as the examiner for a candidate from that delivery. A consultancy that trains a client does not certify that client's people. Those candidates are examined by someone who did not train them.

This separation is the licensing model.

The text of the Flux standard is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). You may copy it and adapt it. Attribution is required.

The marks are not in that license. "Flux" used as a credential, the tier titles used as a credential, and the credential ids are controlled by the Certification Board. Teaching the terms is allowed. Issuing a Flux credential is not, unless the Board has licensed the examination.

The Flux tool is a separate work. Using the tool does not grant a mark. Holding a mark does not assign any right in the tool.
