**Executive sponsor.** Signs the Big Pause and the board report. Does not edit a unit to move a number.

**Organizational steward.** Keeps the registers, the learner map, and the session practice. Signs the Big Pause with the sponsor.

**Practitioner.** Writes units and keeps them accurate under the standard.

**Master.** Reviews judgment: what to accept, what to leave as debt, and when a unit is superseded. The Master mark does not require the Steward mark.

**Trainer.** Teaches a course under a license. A trainer does not issue a mark.

**Examiner.** Grades an assessment for the Certification Board. An examiner does not grade a candidate they trained, and does not grade a candidate trained by their firm in that cohort.

The wider group that only needs the language takes The Flux language course. They do not need a mark to use the terms. Employers and auditors who need to check a mark use [Verify](/verify). They do not need an account.
