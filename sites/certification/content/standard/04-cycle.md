Knowledge enters from outside the organization. It is not yet an asset.

The Big Pause is the gate. The sponsor and the steward sign that the organization will learn the language before it starts the project. Work that skips the gate adds debt before there is a register to put it on.

Past the gate, the organization organizes the knowledge: a unit, a register row, an owner.

A value session is where typed knowledge is captured. A session that does not produce or update a unit did not move the cycle.

Actionable value is the accepted unit in use. Velocity is how many days that turn took. The next piece of outside knowledge starts another turn. Enterprise debt is whatever in the last turn was never organized. It makes the next turn slower.
