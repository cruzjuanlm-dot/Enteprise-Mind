This document is Flux Standard v0.1. Its status is Draft.

Flux uses three labels for the text.

**Draft.** Open for public comment. Wording may change. Do not treat a Draft as the rule an examination will use.

**Candidate.** The Standards Council has frozen the wording for a recorded comment period. A change requires an objection against a section.

**Final.** The Certification Board examines against this wording. A newer Final does not rewrite a mark already issued. Renewal uses the Final in force on the renewal date.

v0.1 is Draft. There is no Candidate and no Final. This prototype is not a basis for an examination.

Comments go to the public issue tracker, from the comment window on this page. An objection to a Draft is a comment. An objection to a Candidate, when one exists, names the section.
