Flux standardizes a language, a cycle, and the records an organization keeps so that knowledge from outside it becomes organized, actionable value.

The standard covers:

- The terms in the next section.
- The value cycle, including velocity, enterprise debt, and the Big Pause.
- The shape of a knowledge unit and of a comp.
- The roles that sign, teach, and examine.
- How a mark is issued, renewed, and revoked.

The standard does not cover a software product. The Flux tool is the register and the proof of concept. The durable asset is the framework, the text, and the marks. An organization may keep the same units in git without running the tool.

Out of scope: a vendor's model, a vendor's meeting product, and any claim that an installation is certified because the tool is present. People hold marks. Installations do not.

Own the framework, the standards, and the certifications. Do not treat the software as the asset.
