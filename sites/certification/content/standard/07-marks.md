Proposed.

A mark is a tier plus a credential id. The public record shows the holder as a name or as initials, the tier, the issue date, the expiry date, and the status: active, expired, or revoked.

Issuing, renewing, and revoking are acts of the Certification Board. They are not git commits.

A revoked mark stays on Verify with status revoked. It is not deleted. An expired mark stays with status expired. Renewal does not rewrite the old dates. Fees are to be announced.

No tier in this prototype is Final. No examination is offered here. The tiers, the blueprints, and the renewal terms are on [Certification](/certification).
